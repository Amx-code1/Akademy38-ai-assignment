Workflow Improvement Challenge

Redesigning the student assessment & feedback cycle using AI

Current system — problems
✕
Time-intensive manual markingA teacher marking 30 essays spends 6–8 hours writing individual feedback by hand — every single assessment cycle.
✕
Inconsistency through fatigueThe 30th paper inevitably receives less detailed, less thoughtful feedback than the 1st. Quality degrades as the teacher tires.
✕
Slow turnaround erodes learningStudents wait days or weeks for feedback, losing the crucial learning moment when the material is still fresh in their minds.
✕
Generic, non-actionable commentsPhrases like "good work" or "needs improvement" give students no roadmap for how to actually get better.

Redesigned AI-powered workflow

📝 Student submits work
→
🤖 AI analyses & scores
→
📊 Draft feedback generated
→
✅ Teacher reviews (2 min)
→
📬 Feedback sent instantly
Tools used in this pipeline: Claude API for personalised feedback generation → Airtable as the student database and submission store → Make.com to automate the pipeline end-to-end → Email/Slack to deliver formatted feedback reports to students within the hour.
Expected benefits
85%Reduction in teacher marking time
<1hrFeedback turnaround time
100%Consistency across all students
3×More personalised feedback per student

Shift in the teacher's role

The AI handles repetitive first-draft feedback. The teacher's role shifts to high-value activities: reviewing AI drafts for accuracy, identifying class-wide patterns (e.g. "20 students struggled with algebra fractions — I need to reteach this"), and spending more time in 1-to-1 mentoring conversations where human judgment genuinely matters.

This is not AI replacing teachers — it is AI handling the administrative grind so teachers can focus on what they do best: inspiring and guiding students.

AI Tool Demonstration

A live, working AI homework helper — powered by Claude

The system below is a functioning AI homework assistant for students aged 10–15. It uses the Claude API with a carefully engineered system prompt: hints-first scaffolded explanations, age-appropriate language, and a Socratic approach that guides students to the answer rather than just giving it to them.

🎓 AKademy38 Homework Helper Powered by Claude AI Ages 10–15
AI
Hi there! 👋 I'm your AKademy38 homework helper. Ask me any Maths or Science question and I'll guide you through it step by step — I'll give you hints first so you can figure it out yourself. What are you working on today?
You
I don't understand algebra. What is 2x + 5 = 13?
AI
Great question — algebra is all about being a detective! 🕵️ Let me give you a hint instead of the answer, so you can feel the satisfaction of solving it yourself.

🔍 Hint 1: Your goal is to get x completely on its own on one side of the equals sign.

Right now, 2x has a +5 beside it. What operation would cancel out that +5? Try that step and tell me what you get!
AI

How this system is architected

🧠 AI backbone

Claude API with an engineered system prompt that enforces: hints-first pedagogy, age-appropriate vocabulary, topic restrictions to educational subjects only, and an encouraging tone at all times.

🖥️ Frontend

HTML/CSS/JS chat interface — mobile-friendly, branded, and embeddable in any school portal or LMS with a single snippet of code.

🛡️ Safety & scope

The system prompt restricts the AI to educational topics only, flags anything off-topic, and is designed to maintain a safe, encouraging environment at all times.

📊 Teacher insights

Airtable logs every question by topic and student. Teachers receive a weekly report showing which concepts were most struggled with — enabling data-driven lesson planning.

🔢 Fractions & Percentages

AKademy38 Education  ·  Ages 10–15  ·  Three levels: warm-up → building up → challenge

📖 How to use this worksheet: Start at Level 1 and work your way up. Read each hint box carefully before you begin that level. Type your answers in the boxes — when you're done, click "Check my answers" at the bottom!
🌱 Level 1 — Warm-up: converting fractions to percentages
💡 Hint: To convert a fraction to a percentage, divide the top number (numerator) by the bottom number (denominator), then multiply by 100. Example: 1/4 = 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25 → 0.25 × 100 = 25%
